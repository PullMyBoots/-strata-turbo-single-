Pelican Riding a Bicycle

A 2D SVG cartoon: helmet-wearing pelican, pedalling a red road bike past scrolling hills, trees and clouds. Drawn with paths only, animated with SMIL (animate / animateTransform).

Keyboard: Space play/pause · R restart · N night · 1/2 speed. The animation runs without JavaScript; the controls just tune it.

Single file, no network requests, no external assets. Geometry is plain SVG paths; motion is SMIL (animateTransform for wheels, cranks, parallax and body bob; animate on path d for the pedalling legs, pouch bulge and wing flex).
